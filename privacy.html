Privacy Policy

P-01 is a private, internal tool used solely by its developer to automatically back up application data to a Google Drive account under the developer's own control.

This application does not collect, process, share, or sell personal data belonging to any third party. It does not have public users. Access to its Google account integration is restricted to the developer's own Google account only.

Data accessed via the Google Drive API (drive.file scope) is limited to files created by this application, used exclusively for automated backup storage.
